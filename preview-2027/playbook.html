THE PLAYBOOK

Micro drama runs on data. We hook people in every part of the story, then measure exactly where they leave.

The method we use on our own shows, and the one we bring to brands.

1 · START WITH THE ENGINE

Before a single scene, we write the story engine in one sentence: a humiliation or a loss, a hidden power, and a reversal the audience knows is coming. Then the core fantasy, the lie everyone believes, and one point-of-view character.

2 · BUILD EVERY EPISODE THE SAME WAY

  • 0 to 3 seconds: the hook. A voice mid-sentence, a slap, a secret.
  • The middle: one escalation. Blunt dialogue, no subtext.
  • The end: a cliffhanger, every single time.
  • The frame: vertical 9:16, 30 to 90 seconds, mostly close-ups, two actors, one room.

3 · SHAPE THE FIRST TEN

  1. Episode 1: the humiliation.
  2. By episode 3: the secret is hinted.
  3. By episode 5: the power shifts.
  4. By episode 10: the big reveal is teased, right before the paywall.

4 · MEASURE EVERY SECOND

Our app, Cliffhanger, records the second each viewer leaves an episode. Every month we publish what we learned here. We only publish real numbers, so this section fills in once the beta has enough viewers.
