THE PREMISE

Victor Aurelion is the most feared man in fragrance. He runs a nine-billion-dollar empire from a locked marble bathroom on the 61st floor, and he will not explain why.

In the first episode his own son votes him out, on speaker, while the board laughs. He has thirty days. And he is not getting up.

ONE ROOM. ONE SHOT. EVERY EPISODE.

  • One locked shot. No cuts, no cutaways, no flashbacks. We never leave his face.
  • Everyone else is a voice on the phone, until the day someone finally walks into the frame.
  • A hook in the first 3 seconds and a cliffhanger at the end. No exceptions.
  • Made for your phone: vertical 9:16, subtitles always on.
