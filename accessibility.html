ACCESSIBILITY

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

Last reviewed November 8, 2026

WHAT'S IN PLACE

  • A full text version. The web résumé has all of the site's content (work, case studies, research, current projects, contact and booking) as a plain page that reflows, zooms to 400% and prints. "Skip intro" is the first link on the arcade page and goes straight there.
  • Keyboard access. Everything in the arcade works with a keyboard: Tab to move, Enter or Space to choose, Escape to back out of a section or the phone view. Focus is always visible.
  • Pause animations. The "Motion" button stops everything that moves on its own: rotating notifications, cabinet screens, the typing effect and the hologram flicker. The site starts paused if your device is set to reduce motion, and it remembers your choice.
  • No flashing. Hologram glitches happen no more than about once every two seconds, well under the three-flashes-per-second limit.
  • Screen readers. Pages use headings, landmarks and labeled controls. Decorative art is hidden from assistive technology, and hidden parts of the arcade can't be reached until they're on screen.
  • Color and contrast. Text was checked against its actual rendered background, including over gradients and effects, to meet 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text). Meaning never depends on color alone; chart bars and statuses are labeled in text.
  • Testing. Automated checks with axe-core across every page and interactive state, rendered-pixel contrast measurement, and manual keyboard testing at desktop and phone sizes.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

  • The arcade page is a fixed-layout illustrated experience that scales to fit your screen, so it doesn't reflow like a normal page when you zoom. The web résumé is the conforming alternate version with the same content and fully supports zoom, text spacing and reflow.
  • Inside the hologram, colors are tinted cyan as a visual effect. All information there is also in text and in the web résumé.
  • Booking goes through Calendly and some links open LinkedIn and GitHub. Those services have their own accessibility.
